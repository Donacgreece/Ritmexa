Privacy by design.

Local media processing

Ritmexa is designed to process selected photos, videos and audio in your browser. Version 0.1 does not upload your selected media to a Ritmexa server.

Local preferences

The app may store lightweight preferences such as language in your browser storage.

Third-party hosting

The website may be delivered by the hosting provider you choose, such as GitHub Pages. Their normal server logs may apply to page requests, but your selected editing media is processed locally by the application.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the project repository contact information.
